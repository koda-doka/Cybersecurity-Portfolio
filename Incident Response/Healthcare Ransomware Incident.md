# Healthcare Incident

A primary-care clinic suffered a ransomware incident that began when several employees opened phishing emails containing malware. Once the attackers gained access to the network, they deployed ransomware that encrypted important files, including patient records. Staff were unable to use their computers and a ransom message appeared demanding payment for a decryption key. The loss of access to essential systems caused the clinic to halt operations and contact external organizations for help responding to the incident.

# Incident Report

**Description:** Documenting a cybersecurity incident.

**Tool(s) used:** None.

**The 5 W's:**
- **Who:** An organized group of unethical hackers.
- **What:** A ransomware security incident.
- **When:** Tuesday, around 9AM
- **Where:** A U.S. health clinic.
- **Why:** This incident occurred when unethical hackers obtained access to the company's system using a phishing attack. Once they entered the network, they launched ransomware on the company's system and encrypted critical files. Their motivation was financial because they displayed a ransom note demanding a large sum of money in exchange for restoring the files.

**Additional Notes:**
The health care company needs to educate its staff on common phishing emails as well as enforce domain-level restrictions. Following security best practices and conducting regular security audits will improve their overall security posture.
