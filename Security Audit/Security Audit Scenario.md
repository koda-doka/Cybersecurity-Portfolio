# Security Audit Scenario
This project is a mock security audit based on a fictional toy company that operates out of a single locaiton, but has recently expanded its online sales to customers around the world. As the business grows, their small IT team is struggling to keep up with new security compliance demands.

The IT manager wants to understand the company's current security posture and identify weaknesses that could affect daily operations, regulatory compliance, or online payment processing. To do this, they begin an internal review using the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. They define an audit scope, outline goals, list key assets, and perform an initial risk assessment.

My task was to examin the manager's documentation and complete an internal controls and compliance checklist. The goal was to evaluate how well the organization is managing risks and where improvements may be needed.
